Personal workflow automation (n8n), built for my own use.

This site is the home page for "GSheets", a private Google OAuth app that connects my self-hosted n8n instance to my own Google Calendar, Google Sheets and Gmail. It is used only by me, to run personal productivity workflows such as a weekly task digest.

It is not a public service and does not accept sign-ups.
