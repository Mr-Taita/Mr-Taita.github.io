Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Who uses this app

"GSheets" is a private Google OAuth app used only by its owner, Taita Iliya, to connect a self-hosted n8n automation instance to the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent, the app accesses: Google Calendar events (read), Google Sheets the owner selects (read and write), and Gmail (to send emails to the owner).

How the data is used

Data is used only to run the owner's own automations, for example compiling a weekly digest of tasks and calendar events and emailing it to the owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Where the data is stored

Workflows run on the owner's own computer. Google access tokens are stored locally in that n8n instance. No data is stored on this website.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.

Contact

Use the support contact shown on the Google consent screen for this app.
